# Public-Source Data Contribution Policy

## Allowed contribution

A data-source suggestion may contain only:

- a direct URL to a lawfully accessible public source;
- factual metadata such as publisher, title, publication date, language,
  region, and source type;
- a short original summary written by the contributor;
- a concise explanation of relevance and known limitations; and
- license, reuse, or provenance information when it is available.

Link to the source instead of copying it. Use the dedicated issue form and the
minimum information required for review.

## Prohibited content

Do not submit, paste, upload, embed, or link to:

- third-party full text, article archives, copyrighted document copies, or
  substantial excerpts;
- paywalled, subscription-only, leaked, scraped-behind-login, or access-control
  bypassed content;
- account screenshots, QR codes, login links, session material, private
  messages, or email headers;
- personal data, contact details, biometric data, precise private locations,
  user exports, or other personally identifiable information;
- credentials, tokens, private keys, real configuration, environment files,
  database dumps, telemetry, or raw logs;
- classified, export-controlled, unlawfully obtained, suspected internal, or
  otherwise sensitive non-public material; or
- executable files, macros, archives, opaque attachments, or local filesystem
  paths.

When source status is uncertain, do not submit it. Ask a source-policy question
without including the material.

## Review rules

Reviewers verify that the URL is public, the metadata is factual, the summary
is original and proportionate, and no prohibited content is present. A public
URL alone does not establish accuracy, ownership, or redistribution rights.
Reviewers may redact or close a submission and must not download suspicious
attachments or attempt to bypass access controls.

Accepted metadata must retain provenance to the original URL. Corrections and
removal requests should identify the repository record, explain the issue, and
avoid repeating personal or restricted content.

## Security and privacy

Potential vulnerabilities go through private security reporting, not a data
issue. If personal or sensitive information is accidentally posted, stop
sharing it, use GitHub's report/moderation tools, and notify maintainers with a
link only; do not quote the content elsewhere.

## Honest boundaries

- Review cannot guarantee that a public source is accurate, unbiased, lawful
  in every jurisdiction, or permanently available.
- Metadata and an original summary can still reveal sensitive context when
  combined with other data.
- Repository maintainers cannot adjudicate ownership, classification, export
  control, privacy, or fair-use questions for contributors.
- Removal from this repository cannot erase forks, caches, search indexes, or
  copies held by third parties.
