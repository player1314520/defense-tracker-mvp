# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in DefenseTracker to be respectful, technically
rigorous, and safe. Community spaces should be free from harassment,
intimidation, discrimination, doxxing, and pressure to disclose private or
sensitive information.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect copyright, privacy, source restrictions, and security boundaries.
- Use synthetic examples and the minimum information needed to reproduce an
  issue.
- Accept moderator decisions intended to protect contributors or the project.
- Correct mistakes transparently and disclose conflicts of interest that could
  affect a contribution.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Publishing or requesting personal data, credentials, account material,
  private messages, screenshots, QR codes, or location/contact information.
- Sharing classified, suspected internal, unlawfully obtained, or paywalled
  material.
- Coordinated manipulation, impersonation, spam, undisclosed promotion, or
  fabricated evidence.
- Publicly disclosing a vulnerability before using the private reporting path.
- Retaliation against a reporter, reviewer, or moderator.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release
coordination, and project-related public interactions. Maintainers may edit,
hide, lock, or remove content; limit participation; or ban an account when
needed to protect the community.

For a non-sensitive incident, use a moderation request issue and provide only
the public link and a minimal description. For imminent threats or platform
abuse, use GitHub's report/block mechanisms. Security vulnerabilities follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The project does not yet have a dedicated confidential conduct-reporting
channel. Do not place private evidence or personal details in a public issue.
Until a reviewed confidential channel exists, maintainers cannot promise
private intake, anonymity, an appeal panel, or a response deadline.

## Enforcement principles

Moderation should be proportional, documented where privacy permits, and based
on behavior in scope. A maintainer involved in an incident should recuse when
another qualified maintainer is available. Permanent exclusions and reversals
should be recorded without republishing harmful or identifying material.

## Honest boundaries

- This policy is not a contract, legal advice, or a guarantee of safety.
- A single-maintainer project cannot provide fully independent moderation or
  appeals.
- GitHub moderation tools cannot prevent all off-platform harassment or
  re-publication.
- Public records may need redaction, so not every enforcement detail can be
  disclosed.
