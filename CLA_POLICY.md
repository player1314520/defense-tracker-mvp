# Contributor License Agreement Policy

## Current status: INACTIVE — EXTERNAL MERGES BLOCKED

No Individual CLA or Corporate CLA has completed legal review, and no verified
legal steward has been published. Therefore:

- external code, documentation, tests, designs, and other copyrightable pull
  request content must not be merged or cherry-picked;
- checking a box, commenting “I agree,” signing a commit, or opening a pull
  request is not an effective CLA for this project;
- maintainers must not describe any contributor as having assigned rights or
  granted commercial relicensing authority; and
- automated CLA status checks, if later added, must remain non-authoritative
  until the legal steward formally activates the reviewed texts and process.

External proposals can still be discussed. Bug reports should use synthetic
data, and public-source suggestions must follow the data contribution policy.
Maintainers may independently implement an uncopyrightable idea, but must not
copy blocked pull-request expression.

## Activation requirements

The CLA gate can become active only after all of the following are public and
reviewed:

1. the exact legal name and authority of the project steward;
2. final Individual and Corporate CLA texts reviewed for the relevant
   jurisdictions and the AGPL/commercial dual-license model;
3. an auditable signer-identity and consent process with data-retention and
   privacy notices;
4. a method to verify employer authority for Corporate CLA signatures;
5. documented revocation, correction, access-control, and audit procedures;
   and
6. a required status check tied to the exact pull request commit.

Activation requires a dedicated governance change. It must not be inferred from
a release, README edit, or repository setting.

## Future merge rule

After activation, every external contributor must have a verified applicable
CLA before merge. Contributions remain subject to technical review, license
compatibility, provenance, privacy, security, and CI gates. A CLA is necessary
but never sufficient for acceptance.

## Honest boundaries

- This policy is not an Individual CLA, Corporate CLA, copyright assignment,
  or legal advice.
- It cannot determine whether an employer or contributor owns a proposed
  contribution.
- It does not grant the project a commercial relicensing right.
- Blocking external merges reduces legal ambiguity but also limits community
  development until a valid process exists.
